Voice

Voice output is only started by you — it never plays on its own. Prediction summaries read when you press Listen to result (backend voice /speech/speak, WAV, with a browser fallback). Turning voice off cancels any backend playback and the page reader.

The Read page button in the top bar uses the browser's own speech synthesis (not the backend) to read the whole active page when you click it, with Pause/Resume and Stop. Each card, chart and result also has its own speaker button to read just that section.

A small speaker button on each card, chart and insight turn (Read this section aloud) reads only that section. Pause/Resume and Stop apply to whichever reading is active.
