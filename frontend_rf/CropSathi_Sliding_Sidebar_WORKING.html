Crop knowledge

Quick field guides to help you observe crop health.

🍂 Early blight

Often appears as brown spots with concentric rings on older leaves. Similar symptoms can have other causes, so inspect the whole plant.

Potato care

💧 Watering basics

Water near the soil, avoid prolonged leaf wetness when possible, and adapt watering to soil and weather conditions.

Daily practice

🔎 Regular inspection

Look under leaves for insects and check for spots, curling, yellowing, or sudden wilting.

Prevention

🌱 Healthy soil

Good drainage and balanced nutrients support healthy roots. Avoid adding fertilizer without understanding the crop’s needs.

Using diagnosis

Take a sharp photo in natural light, avoid heavy shadows, and include the affected leaf. Treat any automated result as a preliminary clue, not a confirmed diagnosis.

Need expert advice?

For severe or spreading crop damage, consult a local agricultural extension officer or plant-health specialist.
